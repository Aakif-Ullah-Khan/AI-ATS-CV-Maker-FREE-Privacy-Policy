Section 03

AI Processing of Your Data

This app uses an AI model to generate resume content from the information you provide. Here is exactly what happens with your data during AI processing:

What data is sent to the AI
Your name, email, phone number, career purpose, work experience summary, education history, certifications, languages, and project descriptions are compiled into a prompt and sent to the AI model to generate your resume.
Which AI service is used
We use the LongCat AI API (LongCat Flash Chat model) hosted at api.longcat.chat. This is an Anthropic-compatible API. Your data is sent to their servers for processing.
Data retention by AI provider
We do not control how the LongCat AI API stores or processes prompts on their servers. We recommend reviewing LongCat's own privacy policy for details on their data handling practices.
AI Chat Assistant
When you use the in-editor AI chat to refine your resume, your current resume content and your chat messages are sent to the AI API for processing.
⚠️
Please avoid entering highly sensitive personal information (such as national ID numbers, passport numbers, or financial details) into the resume form. The app is designed for professional career information only.

Data Storage & Security

Data Where Stored Security
Account credentials Supabase cloud (PostgreSQL) Passwords hashed, JWT sessions
Profile information Supabase cloud database Encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
Saved resumes Device local storage (AsyncStorage) Stored on your device only

Section 08

Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide the service:

  • Account & profile data — Retained while your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion request.
  • Saved resumes — Stored locally on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • Daily usage counters — Reset automatically every calendar day. Stored locally only.
  • Exported files (PDF/Word) — Stored in device cache temporarily during sharing. Not retained by us.
